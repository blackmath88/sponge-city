# Comparator selection

Selection rubric (applied before drafting): official primary sources · published monitoring · public data access · reported outcomes · contrast with Basel · transfer relevance. Ratings come from the research workers' own reading of fetched sources on 2026-10-10 (`yes`/`partial`/`no`); they are not scores and are not summed.

| Criterion | Berlin | Copenhagen |
|---|---|---|
| Official primary sources | **yes** — Entries rest on Senate, Umweltatlas, Abgeordnetenhaus and district pages fetched on 2026-10-10. | **yes** — Plan (2012), annual climate-adaptation report (2021), committee minutes and the open data portal are all City of Copenhagen publications. |
| Published monitoring | **partial** — City-wide sealing mapping and modelled water balance are published; no published measured infiltration or retention monitoring was found. | **partial** — Annual programme status is published; no measured performance monitoring of built projects in rain events was found, and heat data are acknowledged as lacking. |
| Public data access | **partial** — Open downloads and a Zero 2.0 licence were confirmed for the 2005 water-balance record only; 2022 licence and the daten.berlin.de record (blocked, HTTP 403) were not verified. | **partial** — Six skybrud datasets under CC BY 4.0 with CKAN API, but they hold plan and status data; flood scenarios are PDFs from 2012 calculations. |
| Reported outcomes | **partial** — Output statistics (19,640 m2 green roof, about 300,000 m3 storage) are reported; effects on runoff, evaporation or overflows are not quantified. | **partial** — Programme-level figures exist (7-9 % risk reduction, 4 of 48 critical areas) with method not stated; no project-level measured outcomes found. |

## Why these two

- **Berlin** was a candidate from earlier repository research (Umweltatlas, QTrees). It contrasts with Basel in scale and in publishing city-wide sealing and a modelled water balance as open maps. It is a comparator for data access and programme steering, not for measured effect.
- **Copenhagen** contrasts with Basel in a flood-led, city-wide plan with a stated target and openly published plan data, in flat terrain with mostly combined sewers. It is a comparator for planning and finance structure, not for measured performance.

## Considered, not chosen

- **Zürich**: the most transferable comparator for Swiss law, climate and German-language documentation. Not researched in this session (time budget), so no source quality was assessed. Recommended as the first addition: the profile schema is reusable.
- **Rotterdam, Singapore, Stockholm and others** appear only as single project facts in `data/sponge-facts.json`; no city-level profile was attempted.

## Known weaknesses (from the workers)

**Berlin**
- Water balance, hazard maps and climate map are modelled; no measured validation results were read (only the titles of ABIMO comparison figures exist on the download page).
- Licence for Wasserhaushalt 2022 and the Versiegelung 2021 datasets was not verified; search snippets for 2017 records gave conflicting licences (BY 2.0 versus Zero 2.0) and were not fetched.
- The sewer storage target is worded inconsistently across Senate pages (over 300,000 m3 additional versus 300,000 m3 total including about 235,000 existing); the 2026 press release says about 300,000 m3 additional created.
- The Senate report 19/3340 was read as text for the Beschluss and the first sections only; later sections were not fully reviewed.
- Several figures (48 and 5.5 million m3 per year) come from administration pages without stated method or year.
- Regenwasseragentur pages were not analysed beyond the home page; BWB primary documents were not fetched.

**Copenhagen**
- Programme risk-reduction figure has no stated method in the passage read.
- Flood scenario maps date from 2012 calculations and are PDF only.
- No measured post-event performance evidence found for any project.
- Heat dimension: the city itself reports no planning-grade data basis (2024).
- 2021 national rules lowered the service level in parts of the city, so the 2012 target is not city-wide current policy.
- Searches covered kk.dk, opendata.dk and open web only; HOFOR pages were not retrievable (404) and its data were not checked.
- The 2012 plan PDF was read from the Climate-ADAPT mirror, not from kk.dk.

## Basel

The Basel profile (`data/cities/basel.json`) is generated by `scripts/build-basel-profile.mjs` from repository evidence (`data/sponge-facts.json`, the Data Charter). Source pages were **not** re-fetched in this session; quotes are the repository's recorded statements (`quote_basis: repository-statement`).

## Comparability

Only one measure pair has a shared topic: sealed share of area (Basel cadastral land cover vs Berlin hybrid ALKIS/satellite mapping). They differ in quantity definition, method and spatial scale, so the page shows them as **not comparable**. Copenhagen's sources gave no measure with quantity, unit, method and scale together, so none is recorded.
