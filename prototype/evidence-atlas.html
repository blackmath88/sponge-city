02 · Know the boundary

“Potential effect” is four different claims.

VoltaNord matters because it can move selected claims from expected to observed. It cannot automatically make them transferable.

1

Mechanism supported

Research or precedent explains how an intervention can work under stated conditions.

2

Effect expected here

The upstream Canvas finds need, opportunity and a compatible mechanism at a candidate site.

3

Effect observed

Monitoring records what happened at an implemented place. This is VoltaNord’s ground-truth role for the four published indicators.

4

Transfer assessed

A named reviewer checks whether design, soil, weather, operation and maintenance are comparable elsewhere.
