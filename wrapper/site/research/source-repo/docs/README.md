# Research & resources

This directory is the team's shared research library for Hack am Rhein.

It is intentionally **not organised around a chosen product idea**. The goal is to preserve useful domain knowledge, data sources, technical patterns, open questions and reusable prior art while the team is still exploring.

## How to use this section

- Start with [challenge landscape](CHALLENGE_LANDSCAPE.md) for neutral notes across the four challenges.
- Use [data & source catalogue](DATA_AND_SOURCES.md) when looking for evidence or APIs.
- Use [technical patterns](TECHNICAL_PATTERNS.md) when a problem starts to look like rules, constraints, provenance, spatial joins or replay.
- Read [research method](RESEARCH_METHOD.md) before promoting a research claim into a build decision.
- The machine-readable link inventory lives in [`resources/catalog.yml`](../resources/catalog.yml).

## Research status labels

We keep research status visible:

- **VERIFIED** — checked against a primary or official source in the research pass.
- **INTERPRETATION** — a reasoned framing or architectural connection, not a sourced fact.
- **SPECULATIVE** — worth exploring, but not yet established.
- **VERIFY** — source/repository exists in our notes but details such as licence, maintenance or exact applicability still need checking.

These labels are not quality scores. They tell us what kind of statement we are looking at.

## Principle

> Research should expand the option space before it narrows it.

A source can be useful even when it disproves a tempting idea. A repo can be useful for one primitive without dictating the product. A shared technical pattern does not mean the challenges need one universal engine.
