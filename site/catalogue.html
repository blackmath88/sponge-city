Unseal street space

Take out asphalt that traffic does not need; replace it with loosened soil and planting.

Basel · Under construction, being piloted or being planned — About 1,000 m² unsealed and about 600 m² of new green space at Sevogelstrasse, from spring 2026.

Unseal courtyards and forecourts

Private and public grounds: replace paving in courtyards, front gardens and yards with planting that takes roof and yard water.

Green roof

A planted layer on flat roofs that holds back and evaporates part of the rain.

Basel · A rule that applies today — § 72 BPG: flat roofs of 10 m² or more must be greened when built or renovated (at least 15 cm substrate).

bpg72 ↗

Basel · Announced; depends on a pending decision — Funding for roof greening beyond the minimum, planned from 2026.
