    This is an explanatory scenario, not a hydraulic or thermal model. The starting water balance is adapted from general US EPA land-cover figures; intervention changes are deliberately illustrative and show direction only. They do not predict the performance, temperature or overflow risk of a real Basel street. Every sponge has a capacity: in a cloudburst conventional drainage and a designed overflow are still needed. The timeline is one suggested path; the catalog lets you try any order.
